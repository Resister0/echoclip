Echo - lightweight game clipping
https://echoclip.link

PRIVACY
Echo keeps your clips, screenshots and settings on this PC. The only thing it does by itself is check echoclip.link every few hours for a newer version; that check contains only Echo's version number and your system type (Windows or Linux).

Nothing else leaves your PC unless you choose it: connecting a cloud (Echo Cloud, Google Drive or your own storage), making a share link, sending feedback or sending a log.

Full privacy policy: https://echoclip.link/privacy
Code signing policy: https://echoclip.link/code-signing
