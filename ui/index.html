Capture Hotkeys

Each hotkey saves a different amount of the moments you just played. Click a hotkey to change it.

Your keys go through KDE's shortcut system: KDE asks once to confirm them. To change a key later, use System Settings → Keyboard → Shortcuts → Echo (picking keys here only sets the suggestion).

Clips & Storage

Where clips are saved and how much space they may use.

Storage

Clips folder
Storage limitWhen full, the oldest clips go to the Recycle Bin
Auto-delete old clipsClips older than this go to the Recycle Bin (checked at start and every few hours). Favourites are always kept.
Shrink old clipsWhile the PC is idle and no game runs, clips older than this are re-encoded at a steady quality: calm clips shrink a lot, busy ones less, and clips that wouldn't get at least 15% smaller are left alone. Each clip once; favourites stay as they are.
Shrink with AV1Your graphics card can make AV1 files: the same quality at about half the size. Copies, share links and cloud uploads still go out as H.264, so they play on every phone and PC.
Name clips after the game"Valorant 2026-10-31 …" instead of "Clip 2026-10-31 …", based on the window in front when you press the hotkey
Game foldersSave each game's clips in its own folder (…\Valorant\…). Clips outside a game stay in the main folder.
Sort older clips into game foldersMoves clips with a game in their name ("FiveM 2026-…", "fivem_chase.mp4", "GTA V - Moments") from the main folder and "Exported Clips" into that game's folder. Imports do this by themselves.
Copy button copiesClips bigger than the size are shrunk (cached copy, the original is kept). The small arrow next to Copy, or Ctrl+click, lets you pick each time.
WatermarkAn animated Echo logo in the bottom-right corner of editor exports, Discord-size copies, montages and GIFs, and on share link pages. Your recorded clips and full-size copies stay clean.
Discord limits (MB)Free / Nitro Basic / Nitro / Custom - change them if Discord does
