Why Echo is lighter

A native app

Echo 2.0 is written in Rust and draws its own window. There is no browser engine inside it, so it opens fast and stays small.

Your graphics card does the work

Recording runs on the video encoder of your NVIDIA, AMD or Intel card, so your games keep their frames.

Only what you need

No feed, no ads and no account to save a clip. Echo sits in the tray until you press the key.

Bring your Medal clips along

Echo finds your Medal folder and copies the clips over in one click. They keep their original dates and game folders, and your Medal files stay where they are. In Echo: Settings → Clips & Storage → Bring in clips from other apps.
