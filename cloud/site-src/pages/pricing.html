Do I need Echo Cloud?

No. Clips are always saved on your PC. For backups you can connect Google Drive, MEGA, pCloud, Filen, your own WebDAV/SFTP/S3 server or a NAS instead, all with free options. When you've connected your own cloud, backups go there - Echo Cloud is only used for the share links that play inside Discord.
