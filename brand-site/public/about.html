About Brandname

Free gaming tools by one independent developer

Brandname is a small family of free PC gaming tools, made by Resister0, one independent developer. It started with Echo, a game clip recorder, and grows one tool at a time.

The goal: an app that runs next to your game should cost it nothing. So the apps are native (built in Rust, without a bundled web browser), they do one job each, and they get out of the way while you play.

The apps

  • Echo - game clip recorder for Windows and Linux. Out now: echoclip.link
  • Brandname Crosshair - a crosshair overlay for any game. Coming soon.
  • Brandname Snip - a screenshot and snipping tool. Coming soon.

Free and open source

The apps are free, with no ads and no tracking. The code is public on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 licence. Echo Cloud, Echo's optional clip storage, has paid plans for people who want more space; that is what pays for the servers.
